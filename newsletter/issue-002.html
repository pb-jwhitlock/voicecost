VoiceCost #2: Deepgram's Nova-3 promo is gone, so our example call rose to $0.0945/min

Hi, it's John with this week's VoiceCost update.

Short version: a quiet stretch, then a busy two days. VoiceCost checked every tracked vendor's pricing page each day from October 20 to October 29. Through October 27 the only movement was exchange-rate drift. Then on October 28 and 29 several vendors changed their pricing pages: one promotion ended, one sale started, one new voice launched, and several products are retiring or already gone. We now track 24 vendors and 66 price lines.

What moved

  • Deepgram Nova-3 streaming: $0.0048 → $0.0077/min. Through October 28, Deepgram's page showed Nova-3 Monolingual at a limited-time "current price" of $0.0048/min next to a "regular price" of $0.0077. Its redesigned page now lists a single pay-as-you-go rate of $0.0077/min. Flux English streaming went from $0.0065 to $0.0077 the same way.
  • New: AssemblyAI text-to-speech, $15 per 1M characters. AssemblyAI-TTS-1 launched October 27. That's the same list price as OpenAI's tts-1, and well under ElevenLabs Flash / Turbo at $40.
  • Cartesia managed voice agents: on sale at $0.03/min. This is a promotional price through December 31, 2026. The regular price on the page is still $0.06/min. Telephony ($0.014/min on Cartesia numbers) and LLM usage are billed on top.
  • Speechmatics pre-recorded: the page now shows list rates. Melia 1 is listed at $0.24/hr, Enhanced at $0.75/hr and Standard at $0.45/hr, roughly double what the page showed last week ($0.12, $0.38 and $0.23). The page now labels these as list rates before its 33% model-training discount and volume discounts of up to 25%, so check which rate you actually qualify for.
  • Exchange-rate drift: Gladia charges in euros (€0.68/hr real-time, €0.16/hr Solaria-3 async, both unchanged). In dollars, real-time slipped from $0.012710/min on Oct 20 to $0.012617, and Solaria-3 async from $0.002991 to $0.002969, under 1%.

Every daily entry is in the changes log.

This week's tip: watch the LLM line

Once you've picked a platform, the LLM is the piece that swings your cost the most. The per-minute LLM rates Retell publishes run from $0.008 (GPT 5 mini) to $0.045 (GPT 4.1), with Gemini 3.0 Flash at $0.016 and Claude 4.5 haiku at $0.025 in between.

Keep everything else in the stack above the same and only swap the model:

  • GPT 5 mini: $0.0897/min, or about $0.45 for a 5-minute call.
  • GPT 4.1 mini (our example): $0.0945/min, or about $0.47.
  • GPT 4.1: $0.1267/min, or about $0.63.

That's a 41% jump in cost per minute from the model choice alone. The $0.037 spread between those LLM rates is wider than the range for any other piece we track once the platform is set. Test the cheapest model that handles your calls well before you upgrade. (Vapi passes model costs through at cost, so your LLM line there may differ from Retell's list price.) Try your own mix in the calculator, or compare platforms with Vapi vs Retell.
